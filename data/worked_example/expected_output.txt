    (a + b + c^2) . (e + a^3)

    as printed by the implementation:  [C] (a+b+c^2) . (e+a^3)
    ([C] is the childhood tier marker; ages 0-12 are childhood, so the 12-16
    period is also [C].)

Symbol assignments:
    a = q_a = Emotional abuse   -> ACE-1
    b = q_b = Felt unloved      -> ACE-4
    c = q_c = Peer bullying     -> ACE-11
    e = q_e = Physical abuse    -> ACE-2

Why each exponent is what it is:
    c^2  -- the bullying sub-event's own text says "frequently", explicit
             repetition language, so the self-loop fires: 1 -> 2. The same
             word does not raise a or b, although all three come from one
             note line: repetition is read from each sub-event's own quote.
    a^3  -- emotional abuse returns at 12-16 after a gap (absent 9-11), the
             same cluster recurring in a later, disjoint span: one feedback,
             +2 on the reactivated occurrence: 1 -> 3.
    b, e -- one occurrence, no repetition language, no reactivation: 1.

Dropped at projection:
    d (caregiver death, age 11) -- no counterpart among the fourteen items.
    p (depressive symptoms, 12-16) -- an outcome, not an adverse experience.
    adult betrayals, low mood, insomnia -- non-ACE clusters, outside 0-18.

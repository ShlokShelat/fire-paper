[Indian clinical glossary of kinship terms and idioms]
Supplementary Material, Section S4. The table below has the same entries as
INDIAN_CULTURAL_GLOSSARY in src/extraction/fire_stage2_3_single_model.py,
which Stage 3 uses to attach cultural notes to events; the extraction
prompt's system message carries a shorter version of it, and Stages 3-4
resolve kinship abbreviations (mil, fil, sil, ...) through
PERSON_ANCHOR_EXPANSIONS. The structure-guided and knowledge-matched
baselines insert the table, from the "term" line on, into their prompts
verbatim; keep it unchanged.

term                        meaning
----                        -------
kaka                        paternal uncle (father's brother)
mama                        maternal uncle (mother's brother)
chacha                      paternal uncle
maasi                       maternal aunt (mother's sister)
bua                         paternal aunt (father's sister)
nani                        maternal grandmother
dadi                        paternal grandmother
nana                        maternal grandfather
dada                        paternal grandfather
kanyadaan                   ritual of father giving daughter at wedding;
                             paternal absence at kanyadaan is clinically
                             significant
joint family                multi-generational household with shared
                             authority, limited individual autonomy,
                             collective decision-making
came home in a bad state    in Indian clinical notes, frequently implies
                             intoxication or aggressive behaviour
boarding school              in Indian family context, often experienced as
                             abandonment or ejection from family
mil                         mother-in-law
fil                         father-in-law
sil                         sister-in-law or brother-in-law
pind                        ancestral village
izzat                       family honour/reputation; threat to izzat is a
                             major stressor in Indian families
